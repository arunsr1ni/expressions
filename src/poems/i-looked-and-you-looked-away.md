I looked, and you looked away.
I exult at every sunrise, you cower and hide.
I revel in the cold rain's touch, you beg for it to cease.
I marvel at a deer satiating a lion, you flinch in disgust.
I watch a cockroach lift its ancient wings - survivor of extinctions, heir to atoms,
and feel the same awe I gift the butterfly.
I sit with grief and let it speak, you bolt the door and call it strength,
I rejoice at the chance to have made all that memory.
You kneel before a judgment that never comes.
