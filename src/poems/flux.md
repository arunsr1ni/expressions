---
title: "Flux"
date: 2026-05-31
excerpt: "What word is that?"
layout: poem.njk
---

What word is that?
Constantly confused with indecisiveness,
Or worse, betrayal,
Sometimes attributed to self-preservation,
Sometimes to being cunning,
But when the justice of fate rises the gavel,
Its powerlessness is magnified,
So hear me, you scoundrel,
You wastrel, pathetic reason for a being,
When the world turns to dust,
History will remember you,
As the leech of opportunities,
And not a benign survivor of chances,
So be all the flux you can now,
The light at the tunnel will embrace you fast,
Whether it is a portal for peace? You ask?
Definitely the malediction preceding a monster,
An acidic prelude to an arid past,
Where histories of a good few will fail to last.
